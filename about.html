About Doughy Designs

Whatever you've ever wanted to put on a cookie, we've got it.

Our story

Have you ever wanted to put your buddy's face on a cookie and give it to them as a joke? Or maybe you just want to bring a batch of cookies to Grandpa Joe's 80th birthday party that say “Happy Birthday, Grandpa Joe!” That is exactly why Doughy Designs exists.

We are a custom cookie design business. We bake one thing — a simple, classic sugar/butter cookie — and then we let you decide what goes on top of it. Toppings, designs, messages, faces: the cookie is the canvas.

What we specialize in

We specialize in custom-designed cookies. Instead of a long menu of flavors and shapes, we keep one standard cookie so the design is the star, and so your order stays simple from start to finish.

Your design options

There are three ways to design a Doughy Designs cookie, and the price per cookie depends on which one you choose:

  • Pre-made design · $2.00
  • Custom writing · $2.50
  • Custom image · $3.00

Pre-made designs come from our catalog of business-created artwork — stock designs, decorations, fonts, and basic sayings for holidays, birthdays, sports, and plain silliness. Custom writing lets you put your own short message on the cookie. Custom image lets you upload a picture you have permission to use and place it right on the cookie.

Occasions we bake for

Our customers are people who host parties, celebrate milestones, and give gifts that get a laugh. Cookies have gone out for:

  • Birthdays
  • Graduations
  • Holidays
  • Parties
  • Weddings
  • Gifts
  • Sports events
  • Inside jokes
  • Just because

What makes us different

Most bakeries ask you to pick from what they already made. We ask what you want on it. You can go from choosing a design to placing an order in a few steps, bulk pricing applies automatically as your order grows, and shipping is always free — no surprise charges at the end.

The standard cookie

One recipe, every order: a classic sugar/butter cookie with a smooth surface made for images, text, and decorations. No flavor, shape, size, or topping choices to make.
